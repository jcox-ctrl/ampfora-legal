Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Ampfora ("we," "us") is operated by Reach Mobile, LLC and is currently a private beta. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email from Apple or Google Sign In.
  • Your content: photos you capture or upload (including receipts you choose to photograph), the items, values, notes, stories, and target prices you save, and voice notes you record about a piece.
  • Purchase details: if you photograph a receipt, we read the price, date, and place of purchase from it so your records are complete. You can edit or remove these at any time.
  • Addresses and vehicle details: if you file a home, we keep the address you enter so we can show the right local price index and the right lookup links. If you file a car, we keep the VIN and mileage you enter so it can be priced.
  • Corrections: when you correct or confirm an identification, we keep the before-and-after (never including any price you paid) to make identification more accurate.
  • Usage & diagnostics: how the app is used (analytics), crash reports, and basic device info.

We do not collect your contacts or precise location, and we never run facial recognition.

How we use it

To identify and value your pieces, run the app's features, keep it reliable, and improve it. We do not sell your personal information and we show no third-party advertising.

Sharing you start

Nothing in Ampfora is public, and nothing is shared unless you start it:

  • Sharing your collection: if you invite someone, they see the pieces you share. Values are hidden unless you turn them on, and you can revoke access at any time.
  • Duels: a friendly market game. Your rival sees only the name you type for yourself and the market models you both pick. Your collection, your photos, and your values never cross.

Who processes your data

We share only what's needed with providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: hosting, database, storage, and sign-in.
  • Anthropic: AI identification; the photos and item details you submit are sent to generate a result.
  • Remove.bg: background removal when you beautify a photo (the image is sent for that step).
  • eBay: market price lookups (item details only; no personal information is sent).
  • Serper: web search for cited retail references (item names only; no personal information is sent).
  • metals.dev: daily precious-metal prices (no personal information is sent).
  • MarketCheck: car valuations (the VIN, mileage, and a postal code are sent; your name and contact details are not).
  • NHTSA vPIC: decodes a VIN into its year, make, model and trim (the VIN only; a free United States government service).
  • PostHog: product analytics. Sentry: crash diagnostics.

Each is bound to use the data only to provide its service to us.

How it's protected

Data is encrypted in transit. Your photos and voice notes live in private storage, and database access is row-secured: only you can see your data, unless you choose to share it as described above.

Your choices

Remove any item at any time, sign out from the You screen, or delete your account in-app. You can also email us to request a copy of your data.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. On a deletion request we remove your account data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Children

Ampfora is for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

Changes & contact

We'll update this policy as the app evolves and revise the date above. Questions: james@ampfora.com.
